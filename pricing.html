Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Forge has no sign-up, no email and no password. Open the app and start tracking.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. Your habits, workouts, mood and scores are stored and calculated on your device, inside the app's sandbox, protected by iOS encryption. Read the privacy policy for the full detail.

What happens if I change phones?

Because there is no cloud sync, your data lives on the device. You can export everything at any time from Settings → Export Data in the app.

How do I upgrade or cancel Premium?

Premium is bought inside the app through Apple. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel anytime in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Will the price go up as features are added?

No. Every future feature ships to existing subscribers at no extra cost.
